# Frigate business card

US standard size, 3.5 × 2 in, at 300 dpi.

| File | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `frigate-business-card-print.pdf` | Send this to the printer. Page 1 is the front, page 2 the back. Includes a 1/8 in bleed on every side (3.75 × 2.25 in), the standard most printers ask for. |
| `frigate-business-card-front.png` | Front: the Frigate logo only. 1126 × 676 px with bleed. |
| `frigate-business-card-back.png` | Back: map, Capt. Alex Baumann, phone, Instagram, email, website. 1126 × 676 px with bleed. |
| `frigate-business-card-preview.png` | Both sides as they look once trimmed, for sharing or checking. |

Keep anything important at least 1/8 in inside the cut line; the files already do.
The logo and map come from the original card artwork. For the sharpest print, a
vector (SVG, PDF or AI) version of the logo from the designer is best.
